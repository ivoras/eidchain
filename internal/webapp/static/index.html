Your message is signed with your eID card in this browser (PIN2 prompt) and added to the next block.

This is public and permanent. The message and your signing certificate are stored in every copy of the chain and cannot be deleted. The certificate holds your name and your personal ID number (OIB); the explorer hides the number, but anyone who downloads a block can read it.
